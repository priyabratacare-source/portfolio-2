About Me

Priyabrata Patra

B.Tech Civil Engineering Student | Technology Enthusiast | Learning AutoCAD, Revit & BIM

Hello! I am Priyabrata Patra, a first-year B.Tech Civil Engineering student at Hooghly Engineering & Technology College (HETC), India. I am passionate about engineering, construction technology, digital innovation and continuous learning.

My primary interests include civil engineering, structural design, construction, AutoCAD, Revit and Building Information Modeling (BIM). I am exploring how modern engineering tools and digital technologies can improve infrastructure planning, design and practical engineering workflows.

Alongside my engineering studies, I am developing my knowledge of web development, Python, Django, artificial intelligence, GIS, remote sensing and emerging technologies. I enjoy experimenting with technology, building practical projects and learning how software and engineering can work together.

I believe in learning through practical experience, collaboration, problem-solving and exploring new ideas. My goal is to develop strong technical skills and contribute to meaningful engineering and technology-based solutions.
